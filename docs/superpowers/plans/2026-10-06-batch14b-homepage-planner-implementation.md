## Review Focus

1. Restored intake without a trip-type field: preserve known explicit/return endings and unknown endings distinctly, plus the existing receipt; do not apply a fresh default (Task 1).
2. Repeated city chips plus a planning area: remove/edit by entry ID, preserve independent stays and parent intent, and do not create fixed order (Tasks 1–2).
3. Selected Return versus a described/legacy explicit finish: make pending domain failure correctable; accepted review survives unchanged capture/reload, while later conflicting evidence requires fresh review (Tasks 1, 3 and 4).
4. Owner change, late lookup/voice result, or edit during handoff: retain current owner/revision checks, recover newer draft and avoid duplicate trip creation (Tasks 2–4).
5. Start-only dates, long Spanish labels and interrupted input: keep partial draft, keyboard/focus behavior, date meaning and readable mobile controls (Tasks 2–4).
