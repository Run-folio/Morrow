- Iceland, south coast to the north: `/journey/immersive/place-vik-1536.webp`; Andrea Schaffer · CC BY 2.0 · resized and converted to WebP; display crop.

Homepage destinations and route chapters consume the same inventory. Routes catalogue prefers canonical route imagery over a stale provider cache and links to credits. Route Detail reuses the existing hero/stop-image components and attribution anchors. No homepage composition or geometry changed. The public credits page links each original, licence and change notice; attribution remains available on cropped derivatives. Reykjahlíð uses a labelled Mývatn-area landscape, not a claim that the image depicts a specific hotel or village street.

Provider imagery for other routes/attractions remains under its existing lookup/attribution path; it is not the rights evidence for these 18 destination images or four heroes. This audit does not certify unrelated legacy assets.
