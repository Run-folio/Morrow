Classification and complete data-sharing offer
This physical-land refinement is a Derivative Database, not merely a Produced
Work or an unlicensed factual whitelist. Its complete derivative is freely
available at /data/physical-land/components.json. The underlying pinned OSM
extract is /data/physical-land/unguja-osm-full.json. Version and provenance
records are /data/physical-land/source-approval.json and manifest.json.

The combined physical-land data used with this refinement is also offered
under ODbL1.0. The complete unchanged coarse base is available at
/data/physical-land/natural-earth-base.json. Natural Earth data remains public
domain in its own right: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
It is distributed via world-atlas2.0.2 (ISC package licence). The ODbL offer
covers the derived component layer and its combination, without taking away
Natural Earth's underlying public-domain permissions. Existing GeoNames and
OurAirports endpoint data are not modified or incorporated into this layer;
they retain their own source, attribution and licence metadata.

Anyone receiving this application can obtain these data files without signing
in, payment, credentials or a provider API call. They are ordinary public static
files served by the application. They include the complete source and complete
derivative used here, rather than just an offer to supply them on request.
Attribution and this data-sharing location are included in road-reference
warnings and attribution when the refinement permits a provider result.

Source: https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/relation/11105712/full.json
Relation11105712, version10, physical island Unguja. Every outer member is
independently tagged natural=coastline. Administrative/maritime polygons and
nested dependencies are not accepted. Raw hashes, capture timestamps, relation
and member versions/roles and node-version digest are in source-approval.json.
No snapping or simplification. Separate outer shells receive separate stable
component keys. Output hash is in manifest.json.

Regeneration is offline: node --experimental-strip-types
scripts/build-physical-land-refinement.ts. No runtime OSM requests.
Same-component land continuity permits bounded road assessment only; it does
not establish road access, service availability, traffic conditions or duration.
